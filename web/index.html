It plays by itself once it is on. Drag a knob up or down; what it changes shows on the screen.

The panel is the FM-1's own, at its real proportions: grey is what is printed on the device, green is what it does in AURORA. Every button does something; the knobs' names follow the page that is open. On a phone, turn it sideways, or use the phone layout: the same controls, bigger.

ALGORITHM
the scene
SELECT
the scale
PRESETS
the root note; or hold SCL and press a key
KNOB 1–4
the four of the page that is open
HOME · Sky
DENSITY · BRIGHT · SPACE · DRIFT. The aurora alone
FX · Effects
ECHO · BELLS · WIND · DRONE. Ripples, snow, the horizon glowing
ENV · Pad shape
ATTACK · RELEASE · LENGTH · PADS. The envelope across the sky
EDIT · Pad tone
WAVE · DETUNE · RESO · SHIMMER. The waveform on the horizon
LFO · Motion
RATE · SWEEP · WIDTH · TREMOLO. Ribbons of movement
GLO · Drone
TONE · FIFTH · SUB · BREATH. Rings rising from the horizon
SEQ · Machine
CHORDS · RANGE · VOICES · EVOLVE. The scale as a constellation
SCL tap
the keys: PADS, HOLD (a key latches), BELLS
ARP · Drums
tap: drums on, with a new random pattern, or off · hold and turn the knobs: TEMPO · LEVEL · BUSY · SWING
Play · Freeze
the machine plays or waits · the reverb and the sky turn to ice
Save
keep the scene and settings for next time

Computer keyboard: Z–/ S–' Q–O the keys · 1–7 the pages · Tab SCL · ← → scene · ↑ ↓ scale · Space play · Shift freeze · Enter drums

No sound on an iPhone? Turn the ring/silent switch to ring.
